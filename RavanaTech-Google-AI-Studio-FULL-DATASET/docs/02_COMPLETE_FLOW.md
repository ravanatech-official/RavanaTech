# 02 — Complete Visitor Flow

## A. Arrival

### A1 — World map

Full-screen, calm visual. Voice says:

**English:** “Please mark, click or tap your location.”

**Sinhala:** “කරුණාකර ඔබ සිටින ස්ථානය සිතියම මත mark, click හෝ tap කරන්න.”

Country can be selected by map, search, or optional browser geolocation. Geolocation is never mandatory.

### A2 — Language rule

- Sri Lanka → Sinhala first.
- Any other country → English first.
- Manual language switch is always available.
- Language is selected by country, but the visitor can override it at any time.

### A3 — confirmation

“Great. I’ll guide you in Sinhala.” / “Great. I’ll guide you in English.”

Then immediately transition into the founder reception.

---

## B. Founder reception

Full-screen founder presentation using the **real founder image**. Use the portrait for face-forward framing and the full-body image for wider scenes.

The preferred implementation is a founder-recorded voice file, not browser text-to-speech. Browser speech synthesis is only a fallback accessibility mode.

### Sinhala

“ආයුබෝවන්! රාවණා ටෙක් වෙත සාදරයෙන් පිළිගන්නවා. මම ශාන්තප්‍රිය — ඔබගේ Digital Architect. අද ඔබට අවශ්‍ය දේ අපි එක පියවරකින් එක පියවරකට සරලව හඳුනාගමු.”

### English

“Hey there! Welcome to Ravana Tech. I’m Shanthapriya, your Digital Architect. Let’s make this simple — tell me what you came here to achieve, and I’ll guide you from there.”

The original supplied greeting may be retained as an alternate script, but the preferred production script uses the founder's actual name and avoids overloading the first sentence with marketing claims.

### Five primary choices

1. **I need something new**
2. **I already have a website**
3. **Show me what you can build**
4. **I’m not sure what I need**
5. **I want to discuss my project / quotation**

2. Premium look

## H. Ravana Agent at any point

Persistent but quiet “Ask Ravana Agent” action.

The visitor can type or speak in Sinhala or English:
- “What do I need?”
- “Show me a website for my salon.”
- “How much is the starter site?”
- “Can you explain that simply?”
- “I don’t understand this.”
- “Take me back.”

The Agent can navigate, explain, compare, summarize, and prepare the next action. It cannot invent facts, pricing, guarantees, reviews, clients, credentials, or availability.

---

## I. Exit

Before leaving or after a meaningful completion, show a tiny optional pulse:

**“Did Ravana Tech make this easier for you today?”**

1. Not yet
2. A little
3. Yes
4. Very much
5. Exactly what I needed

Optional second question:

**“What would you change?”**

One-line text, optional.

This is not a sales form. It is product research.
