Manifesto — 02
A 1920s villa in Amalfi

We preserved the silence. Everything else followed.

01

In 1927, a Neapolitan engineer built a summer retreat into the cliff between terraces of Sfusato lemons. For ninety years it stood half-abandoned — lime washing away, shutters blue peeling to bare wood.

When we found it in 2019, we didn't renovate. We removed. Three years with local maestri, removing cement added in the 70s, uncovering original pozzolana plaster, re-laying chestnut beams by hand. The result is not a hotel. It is a house that breathes with the sea.
