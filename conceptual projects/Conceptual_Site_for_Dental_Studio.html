02 / Philosophy

We don't create
perfect smiles.
We calibrate
yours.

Dr. Min-ji Yeon, DDS
Founder • Prosthodontics / Yonsei

In Seoul, aesthetic dentistry became a trend. At Yeon, it's a discipline. Every millimeter is measured against your facial ratio, lip dynamics, and speech — not a catalog smile.

01 — Ratio Not Template

We use digital facebow + DSD (Digital Smile Design) to map your smile to golden proportion 1:1.618 adapted to Asian facial aesthetics. No two blueprints are identical.

02 — Material Truth

E.max ceramic, 0.3mm minimally invasive. Translucency calibrated for Korean natural shade 1M1-2M2, not bleached Hollywood white. Light passes like real enamel.
