"Gelato is custard's unruly cousin. We give it 24h cold age, zero stabilizers, and a paddling slower than a bicycle. That’s where the chubby texture comes from."
01

Culture First

Milk from Hoengseong cultured 18 hours with kefir. Adds funk, reduces sweetness, holds scoop.

PH 4.6 — READY
02

Ferment Fruit

Strawberries lacto-fermented 3% salt for 5 days. Jammy depth without extra sugar.

DAY 5 — BUBBLING
03

Waffle Protocol

Rice flour + butter + tiny black sesame. Baked in 140-second press, curled by hand in 9 seconds.

19 min cycle 42 cones / hr
04

No Air Whip.
Just Churn.

Vertical dasher at 34 rpm. We fold air out, not in. Result: dense, stretchy, melts slow.
