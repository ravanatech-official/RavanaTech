(02) — PROCESS

Chemistry
is slow
photography.

The darkroom is not about nostalgia. It is about control. You cannot swipe to undo a print. You burn, you dodge, you watch the paper breathe in the tray at 20°C. The image decides when it wants to appear.

Our enlargers are DeVere 504s. Our lenses are Rodenstock. Our timer is still the GraLab 450 from 1974. It clicks.

01 / EXPOSE
Test strips at 3 sec intervals. Trust your eyes, not the meter.
02 / DEVELOP
Agitate 5s every 30s. No music. Listen to the paper.
03 / FIX & WASH
Archival wash 60 min. The print will outlive us.
[LEICA M SYSTEM]
We shoot what we print.
M6 TTL • M4-P • 35/50/90 Summicron. No autofocus. One frame at a time. You learn to wait for the moment between moments.
[PAPER STOCK]
Fiber, not RC.
Ilford MG FB Warmtone, Hahnemühle Photo Rag, Adox MCC. Each has a voice. Warmtone for skin, Cold for architecture.
[TONING]
Selenium for permanence.
Slight selenium tone — not for look, for 200-year stability. We are making heirlooms, not content.
