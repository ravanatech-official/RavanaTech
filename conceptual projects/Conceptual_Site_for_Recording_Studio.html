The Room — Since 1971

Built for
the take that
matters.

Live Room • Oak + Douglas Fir 48m² • RT60 0.42s

We didn’t renovate a warehouse. We found a room that already knew how to sound. 1971, built for a radio orchestra. The wood stayed.

The walls are 3 layers of reclaimed oak from a demolished school in Lausanne, spaced with air gaps. No drywall. The ceiling is 4.2 meters high—high enough that a drum kit breathes, low enough that a vocal feels intimate. There is a smell when the tubes warm up. Dust, old varnish, hot electrons. Musicians notice it and play differently.

Every piece of gear was chosen because someone cried using it. The Neve 8014 console came from Pathé Marconi Paris. Channel 12 has a scratch on the fader from a Jacques Brel session. We left it. The Studer A80 still has the splice marks from a 1979 Montreux Jazz Festival master. We record to it first, always. Digital is just a safety copy.
