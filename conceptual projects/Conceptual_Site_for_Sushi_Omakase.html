01 / PHILOSOPHY

The counter
is a kitchen, not
a stage.

We built kappo around a single plank of Kiso hinoki, 200 years old, salvaged from a shrine in Nagano. Eight seats, no more.

The wood breathes. It absorbs soy and sake. Over time it darkens where hands rest. We do not varnish it. We let time season it, the way rice is seasoned with akazu.

Every service begins with wiping the counter. Not for cleanliness — for attention. The cloth moves in one direction. Always.

02 / The Chef

Mori Kentaro does not
believe in talent

He spent four years only cooking rice. Not serving it. Just cooking, tasting, discarding. The shop owner said his rice was too “anxious”. He was pressing too hard.

In Kyoto, you learn by cleaning the kombu, not cutting the fish. For two years I only wiped counters and watched how senior chefs held their breath when forming nigiri. — Kentaro Mori, interview 2022
