Our philosophy

Soft is
strong. Slow is
fast enough.

Sōma started as one woman's living room, a borrowed speaker, and a vow to make yoga feel less like performance and more like coming home. Twelve years later, that living room is a canyon sanctuary with limewashed walls, heated hinoki floors, and silence that actually holds you.

◐
Breath before shape
Every class begins with 7 minutes of breath. No flow until nervous system lands.
◑
No levels, just listening
We don't call out advanced variations. The advanced practice is staying kind.
