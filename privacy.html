PRIVACY & DATA GOVERNANCE POLICY

DOCUMENT REFERENCE: RT-COMPLIANCE-PRIVACY-2026 // EFFECTIVE DATE: OCTOBER 2026 // AUDIT STATUS: CERTIFIED

1. OVERVIEW & APPLICABILITY

Ravana Tech (“we”, “our”, or “us”), led by Founder & Lead Systems Architect Shanthapriya Silva, is committed to safeguarding the privacy, confidentiality, and data autonomy of our clients, prospective partners, and web visitors worldwide.

This Privacy Policy establishes our protocols for collecting, storing, processing, and transferring information gathered through our official domains (ravanatec.web.app, ravanatech.com), project dispatch channels, contact APIs, and electronic consultations.

Global Regulatory Alignment: Our architecture strictly conforms to the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - EU 2016/679), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and Sri Lanka Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022 (PDPA).

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We practice strict data minimization: we only request and retain data strictly necessary to estimate, architect, develop, and maintain software solutions.

  • Direct Client Identification: Name, personal or business email address, WhatsApp/telephone contact number, company or organizational entity name.
  • Project Specifications: Technical requirements, target launch timelines, scope notes, and industry category selected on inquiry.
  • Automated Telemetry & Security Logs: IP address, browser user-agent string, network protocol timestamp, and referral origin (strictly utilized to detect DDoS attacks, prevent contact form spam abuse, and guarantee server stability).
  • Cookies & Local Preferences: Client-side storage of screen index, UI dark-mode preference, and document simulation cache via browser localStorage. We do not place third-party advertising or profiling cookies.

3. PURPOSE & LEGAL BASIS FOR DATA PROCESSING

We process your personal information under the following recognized legal grounds:

  • Contractual Execution & Pre-Contractual Inquiry: To provide custom technical scopes, architectural blueprints, cost estimations, and deliver contracted software services.
  • Legitimate Technical Interest: Maintaining sub-second server response times, enforcing API rate limits, auditing system uptime, and preventing unauthorized infiltration.
  • Explicit Client Consent: Provided knowingly when submitting project intake forms or initiating direct WhatsApp/Email transmissions.

4. DATA SECURITY & STORAGE ARCHITECTURE

Ravana Tech deploys multi-tiered industrial defenses to ensure your proprietary project concepts and personal contact details remain impenetrable:

  • In-Transit Encryption: 100% of network traffic is encrypted via Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) with HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS) headers enforced.
  • Restricted Database Access: Production inquiry databases (leads-db.json & Google Cloud Firestore) reside behind strict server-side boundary proxies. Direct public HTTP traversal to internal storage paths is strictly forbidden and monitored.
  • Zero Data Selling: Under no circumstance does Ravana Tech sell, rent, monetize, or disclose client names, emails, or project ideas to third-party data brokers or marketing syndicates.

5. DATA RETENTION & DELETION PROTOCOLS

Inquiry records are retained in active client databases for a maximum duration of 12 months following the date of submission to facilitate ongoing architectural support and contract continuity. Inactive records exceeding this horizon are automatically wiped or pseudonymized.

You possess the unconditional right to request immediate erasure of your records at any time.

6. YOUR LEGAL RIGHTS

Regardless of your geographic location, Ravana Tech accords you full autonomy over your personal information:

  • Right of Access: Request a full, portable copy of all data records linked to your email or identity.
  • Right to Rectification: Request prompt correction of inaccurate contact or business details.
  • Right to Erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”): Demand complete deletion of your submission records from our local and cloud registers.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Limit our handling of your specifications to storage only without dispatch.
