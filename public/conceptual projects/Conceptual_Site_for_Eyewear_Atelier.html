Why no AR coating?

We want reflection. Clear acetate reflects like ice edge. Coating would kill that shadow line we carved.

If you need anti-reflection, we will refer you to a lens lab. Frame stays pure.

◎

Reflection is design

Tortoise note

Mineral pigment settles 0.3mm per hour. We pour, wait 11 minutes, rotate block 7 degrees. Done by hand, not machine. That is why no two patterns match.
