01 — Philosophy

The Nordic
way of
bathing.

We did not build a spa. We carved a place for the ritual that northern cultures have practiced for centuries: deliberate exposure, followed by deliberate rest.

Four stages. Ninety minutes. No hurry. You move from basalt sauna to eucalyptus steam, to onsen, to 4-degree plunge. Then you sit. The body remembers what the mind forgets.

03 — Thermal Journey

You don't
chase calm.
You pass through
heat to find it.

Step 01

Arrive — Disrobe — Breathe

Leave phone at reception. Linen robe, no perfume. Tea room, 10 minutes of nothing.

Step 02

Sauna — 15 min, löyly twice

Basalt holds heat differently. Sweat, then pour water. Birch branches if you like sting.

Step 03

Onsen — 20 min, weightless

Silica water at 40°C. The body forgets gravity. Eyes open, but soft.

Step 04

Cold — 90 seconds maximum

4°C. Enter slowly. Breath holds you, not willpower. Out before you feel brave.
