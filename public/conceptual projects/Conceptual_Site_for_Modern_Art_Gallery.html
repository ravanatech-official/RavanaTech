[02] — Information

DOT
IS A
METHOD.

An institution dedicated to removing everything that is not essential. Founded Tribeca, 2021. Non-collecting. Non-profit. Radical hospitality.

The white cube is not neutral. It is a technology. A filter. An ideology cast in gypsum and LED. DOT exposes this apparatus — we leave the walls bare, the grid visible, the labor unhidden.

[The Protocol]

Every exhibition begins with subtraction. No plinths unless structurally required. No vinyl unless it carries information. No color on walls — only RAL 9016 pure white, matte, 2% sheen. Natural light only from north-facing clerestory. Artificial light at 4000K, CRI 98.

The red dot is not decorative. It is a data point. Sold. Reserved. Archived. It marks where capital meets contemplation.
