01 / Philosophy

Fire
is the
only
sauce.

We don't own a stove. Every fish at Cala meets holm oak and old olive wood — charcoal that burns at 400°C and leaves nothing behind but salt and smoke. The technique is older than the village: Catalan brasa, a grate, a pair of tongs.

The boat leaves at 3am. If the sea doesn't give it, we don't serve it. Our menu is written at 6am with a piece of chalk.
