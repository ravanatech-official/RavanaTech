01 — Philosophy

Precision
is a
discipline,
not a spec.

◎

We cut Geneva waves by hand. A machine can do it in 6 minutes. We take 4 hours. That is the difference.

ANGLAGE — 45° POLISH TOL: 0.008MM
I MICRONS, NOT MILLIMETERS

Our tolerances are 0.002mm. The industry standard is 0.01mm. You will never see it. You will feel it when the crown clicks.

II TIME, SLOWED

A movement is assembled over 23 hours, then left to rest for 7 days before regulation. It must forget the hands that touched it.

III NO LOGO ON DIAL

The dial is lacquered, not printed. The name is on the bridge where only a watchmaker will ever read it. Ego is noise.
