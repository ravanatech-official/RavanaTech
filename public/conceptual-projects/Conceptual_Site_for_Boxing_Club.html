DO NOT PAINT
Ray “Hammer” Jones / 1979-1985 / 44-2 / Still comes Wednesdays

Founded in sweat,
not strategy decks.

1978. Eddie Crown, ex-Navy, ex-contender, rents half a meatpacking cold store. He puts one ring in, one heavy bag, one speed bag that squeaks. No sign out front. Word gets around.

By ’84 we’ve got Golden Gloves champs sleeping on the mats between shifts. By ’91 we’re loan-sharking wraps because the supplier didn’t show. By ’05 Eddie’s gone. His son Daryl takes the keys. Same rules:

01 Wraps on before you walk past the desk. No exceptions.
02 Bag work first. Always. 6 rounds minimum.
03 You cheer for everyone. Or you leave.
04 If you can talk after the last round, you didn’t go hard enough.
