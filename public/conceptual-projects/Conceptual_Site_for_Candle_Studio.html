01 Philosophy — Slow Burn

We do not scent a room.
We hold a moment
until it cools.

Founded in a former rope factory. Two tables, one double boiler, and a notebook that still smells like vetiver from 2018. Every pour is batched to 12 vessels max. Cure is not optional.

Our wax is a living blend — local beeswax folded with organic coconut + a whisper of apricot. It burns lower, slower, leaves a faint honey at the rim. That's the point.

02 Process — Melt / Pour / Cure / Burn

Every vessel is a controlled imperfection.

We pour at 62–65°C to trap scent without cooking it. Wood wicks are cut by hand. Cure in dark. The rest is wick geometry.

01 60°C

Melt

Beeswax + coconut + apricot folded slow. No paraffin, no soy alone — soy burns anxious.

02 ↘

Pour

Low-temp, single pot, 12 vessels. Wood wicks pre-soaked. One maker per batch — no assembly line.

03 72H

Cure

Dark shelf. No draft. Wax contracts, scent binds to fat. We flip vessels once. Patience is the ingredient.

04 ◍

Burn

Wood wick crackle, 2.5mm melt pool. Trim to 1/4". First burn to edge or don't bother lighting again.
