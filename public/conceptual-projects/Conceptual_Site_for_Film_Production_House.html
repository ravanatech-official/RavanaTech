01 / MANIFESTO EST. BROOKLYN 2014

We don't make
content. We make
cinema that breathes.

Pærallax started in a loft above a laundromat in Bushwick. One Bolex, two lights, and a promise: never shoot anything we wouldn't want to watch in a theater with no phone signal.

Ten years later, we work the same way — small crew, long takes, natural light, real locations. Our films have premiered in competition because we treat festivals as conversations, not markets.
