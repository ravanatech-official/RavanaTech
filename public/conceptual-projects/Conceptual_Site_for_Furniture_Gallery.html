Philosophy 01 — Object Permanence

We don't
sell furniture.
We place
histories.

ARCHIVE No.1956 was founded on a simple thesis: the chair remembers who sat in it.

Every curve in our gallery holds a fingerprint of 20th century optimism — when designers believed a single object could make life better.

01 Authentication
Every piece traced to factory, year, and first owner when possible. Papers live with the object.
02 Restoration
We conserve, not refinish. Patina is biography. Our workshop uses only period-correct methods.
03 Pedestal
No showroom stacks. One object, one pedestal, one light. Like Noguchi staged his Akari.
