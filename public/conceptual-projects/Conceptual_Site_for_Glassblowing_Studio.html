BENCH #2 — 2019
Heat teaches humility. You move with it or you burn.
We founded Fornax in an old transformer factory because the ceilings were high enough for the glory hole's breath. For twelve years we've kept one furnace lit, twenty-four hours, three hundred and sixty-five days. It has never gone cold. Glass doesn't remember blueprints, it remembers rhythm.
PHYSICS

At 1,100°C viscosity is honey. At 700°C it's skin. At 500°C it's stone waiting to crack. Our job is to stay between the first two numbers as long as possible, using only breath, gravity and a wet newspaper pad called a jack.

Every piece begins with a gather: iron pipe dipped into crucible, turned, lifted. The weight tells you what it wants to be. A cup wants 400g. A pitcher wants doubt.

PRACTICE

We don't design silhouettes, we choreograph intervals. Inhale — rotate. Exhale — blow. Seven seconds, no more. Then reheat. Then decide. If you hesitate the glass remembers.

Students come thinking it's about control. After day three they understand it's about negotiation with a material that is always cooling, always falling.

The four heats

EACH REHEAT IS A DECISION. WE ALLOW FOUR.
01 / GATHER 1140°C • 12 SEC

Honey on iron

Crucible dip, even rotation, no bubbles unless wanted. The first mass sets everything.

02 / MARVER 980°C • 20 SEC

Cold table truth

Rolled on steel. Shape begins before breath. Chill sets skin, keeps it centered.

03 / BLOW 820°C • 7 SEC

Breath is form

Puff, not gust. You listen to resistance. Glass inflates where heat lives.

04 / ANNEAL 520°C • 14 HRS

Patience, not force

Kiln holds at 520°C, drifts down for fourteen hours. Skip it and it shatters months later.
