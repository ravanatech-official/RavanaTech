WE DIDN'T
BUILD A PARK.
WE DUG A HOLE
AND FORGOT
TO STOP.

In 1998 the city said no permits, no lights, no chance. So we poured at night. Three truckloads of surplus concrete from the 6th Street bridge demo, mixed by hand with river sand from the LA River. No blueprints — just a garden hose for the radius and Tony's dad holding the level.

PRINCIPLE: If you can roll it with a mason's trowel, you can skate it. Every joint is cold. Every coping is pool block salvaged from demolished Encino mansions. That's why it grinds like teeth.

The Factory isn't smooth. It's alive. Hairline cracks hold winter rain. Summer heat expands the slab 3/8" — you hear it pop at 2pm daily. That's our clock. Kids learn to read the ground, not the signs.
