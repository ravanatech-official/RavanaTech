WE STARTED IN
A GARAGE WITH
TWO FOAM BLANKS
& A DREAM.

1972. Marty & June — two art school dropouts with more beach time than sense — started shaping boards in a Venice garage that leaked every time it rained. Which was never, because it's Venice.

Fifty years later we're still here, still hand-shaping every single board that leaves the shop. No CNC, no factories, no buzzwords. Just foam, fiberglass, and a lot of terrible jokes.
