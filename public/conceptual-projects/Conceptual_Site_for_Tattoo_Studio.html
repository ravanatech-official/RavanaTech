[02] — Process

How it
works.

We reply within 72h. No DMs for booking. Use the form. Bring reference, but expect us to redraw it. Deposits non-refundable. Reschedule once.
01 30 min

Consultation

In-shop or video. We talk placement, size, pain, aging. You talk meaning. We measure, photograph, and tell you if it’s a bad idea. Honesty > money.

02 1-2 weeks

Drawing

Custom drawn once. You see it day before session. One revision included if it’s off. We don’t send designs early — they get stolen.

03 Session

Tattoo

No music requests. No bringing 4 friends. 1 guest max. Breaks every 90min. We stop when it’s done, not when you’re done.

04 For life

Healing & After

Second skin 3 days, then unscented lotion. No gym 7 days, no sun 14. Touch-ups free within 3 months if you followed instructions.
