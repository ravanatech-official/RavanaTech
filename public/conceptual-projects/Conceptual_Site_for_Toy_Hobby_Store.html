Our Story • ストーリー

MADE FOR THE KIDS WHO CHECKED THE BOTTOM OF THE BOX.

KIKAI MART started as one folding table outside a Nakano arcade. Yuki traded duplicate M1 Gorgos for ramen vouchers, then for a garage, then for two shops that smell like cardboard and new paint.

We’re not a big-box nostalgia farm. We’re a collector’s field notebook — every stain on a cardback, every factory swirl in sofubi recorded.

Yuki Tanaka × Maya Ruiz
Founders — one hunts in Osaka wholesalers at 5am, the other restores sun-faded boxes with cotton swabs. Together, 24 years of receipts.
