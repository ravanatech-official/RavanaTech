✦ Our Philosophy

Medicine
should feel
like a
hug, not a hurry.

We built Milo & Sage for nervous pets (and nervous people).
Founder note • 2013

Most clinics are loud. Bright lights. Cold steel. It makes sense animals hate going. We flipped it. Warm cream walls that absorb sound, sage that calms the nervous system, floors heated to 78°F because bellies like warm.

Every appointment is 45 minutes minimum. No double booking. If your dog needs to sniff the room for ten minutes — we budget for ten minutes. If your cat wants to stay in her carrier, we examine her inside it.

Our vets are Fear Free & Low Stress Handling certified, but more importantly — they're pet parents who get it. Slow hands. Soft voices. Treats everywhere.

Good questions we get

Transparent fees
Do you see anxious or reactive pets? Really? +

That’s literally why we exist. Separate entrance for cats, patio pre-sniff for dogs, 45-min slots, floor seating, and we chart their fear score every visit and adjust. If they need a break, we pause and give cheese.

Will I be pressured into upsells I don’t need? +

No. We text you an estimate before touching your pet. You approve on your phone. If you say “just essentials today,” we honor it. No guilt.

What about senior pets and end-of-life care? +

Home visits, quality-of-life scoring, and a private sunroom if you want to be together. We partner with a grief counselor who loves animals and has a cat named Biscuit.

How does pricing work? Is parking awful? +

Parking is 2-hour on Sunset and usually fine before 11am. We validate curiosity, not cars. Prices live on our wall and our site. No hidden fees for “medical waste” — we bake that into care.
