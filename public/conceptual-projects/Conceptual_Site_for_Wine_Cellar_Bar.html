01 — Philosophy

Stone
holds

Notes from the
Cellar Book

— Vol. III

We built CAVEAU inside a 19th-century limestone cave not for romance, but for inertia. Constant humidity, absolute dark, a natural 11°C. Bottles do not age here — they become. Every evening we move only six cases to the tasting chamber. No more. The rest stay untouched.

05½ — Terroir & Origins

We source
from cold places
that forgot fame.

Our cellar began with Pauillac. It now lives mostly elsewhere: Jura’s oxidative whites that taste like salted air, Alto Piemonte Nebbiolo picked at 11.5% potential, Mosel Riesling kept 15 years on lees before release.

Each bottle must pass three questions: Does it improve after 5 years? Does it show its place? Would we want to drink it at midnight alone? If not — we don’t buy.
